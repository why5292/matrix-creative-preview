About Matrix.

Design · Production · Installation · Maintenance

Bringing new media art
into everyday life.

Matrix Creative is a new media art team bringing together art and technology. Artists, creatives, curators, designers, programmers and engineers collaborate across disciplines, with people and their experience of space at the centre of the work.

The team combines new media visuals, behavioural analysis and creative programming to develop concepts for IP, spaces, stage sets and exhibitions. Design, fabrication, installation and ongoing care bring those concepts into physical settings.

Our focus

IP & large-scale installations

Characters and themes take three-dimensional form, with attention to proportion, materials, surface detail and their relationship to the surrounding space.

Art in public spaces

Visual presentation, on-site installation and subsequent refurbishment are considered alongside the use and construction needs of public spaces.

Displays, sets & exhibitions

Themed commercial environments, stage sets and exhibitions bring their subjects to life through settings, installations and lighting.

Founder

John Lau

Stage set design · Commercial art direction · Multimedia creation

John Lau is the founder of Matrix Creative, an experienced stage set designer, television commercial art director, multimedia creator, and designer and art project planner for international themed attractions.

Since 2013, his work has included international theme park and attraction design engineering, themed commercial spaces, high-end retail window displays, and multimedia art creation, planning and supervision. These projects span a range of themes, settings and presentation formats.

More recently, his practice has expanded into spatial projects combining themed design and art, alongside immersive digital content creation and exhibition curation.

Project experience across regions

Projects John has been involved in span Beijing, Shanghai, Hong Kong, Tokyo, Osaka, Seoul, New York, London and Sweden, bringing experience across different locations and creative settings.

Personal client experience

John’s past client experience includes MTR Corporation, H&M, Calvin Klein, Pandora, Hong Kong Disneyland, Beijing Universal Studios and Shiseido, among others.
